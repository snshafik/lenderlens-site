How the pilot works

  1. Fit and scope: A short conversation confirms the business unit, channels, data fields, and intended decisions.
  2. Secure data handoff: You provide aggregate or properly de-identified exports through an agreed secure channel. Do not email borrower names, loan-level identifiers, SSNs, or other consumer personal information.
  3. Analysis: We audit the available funnel metrics and document assumptions and data limitations.
  4. Delivery: Receive the findings PDF plus a review call. Timing is agreed after usable data is confirmed; approximately seven business days for a standard scope.

This pilot does not include ongoing integrations, dashboard hosting, CRM implementation, or guaranteed revenue improvements. Incomplete data may limit funded-loan attribution. Monthly monitoring and integrations are separately scoped after the pilot.
